Hindu Sacred Literature

Hindu scriptures are broadly divided into:

  • Śruti ("heard")
  • Smṛti ("remembered")

Vedas

Oldest Hindu scriptures; foundational ritual and philosophical texts.

Details

Date: c. 1500–500 BCE

Traditionally Revealed To: Ancient Rishis (sages)

Includes:

  • Rigveda
  • Samaveda
  • Yajurveda
  • Atharvaveda

Upanishads

Philosophical texts exploring Brahman, Atman, consciousness, and liberation.

Details

Date: c. 800–200 BCE

Contributors: Various sages including Yajnavalkya and Uddalaka

Major Upanishads include:

  • Isha
  • Kena
  • Katha
  • Mundaka
  • Chandogya
  • Brihadaranyaka
